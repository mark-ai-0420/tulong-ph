Implement Feature 3.8: DOH Botika / Hospital Pharmacy Generic Equivalent Preparation Guide on `/`:

1. **Data Catalog & Pure Domain Logic (`src/lib/data/genericPrescriptionGuide.ts`)**:
   - Model types:
     * `GenericMedicineItem`:
       - `id: string`
       - `genericName: string` (INN)
       - `brandExamples: string[]` (Philippine market brand names)
       - `therapeuticCategory: 'antibiotic' | 'cardiovascular' | 'diabetes_endocrine' | 'pain_analgesic' | 'respiratory' | 'gastrointestinal' | 'critical_inpatient' | 'other'`
       - `commonDosageForms: string[]` (e.g. "500 mg tablet", "1 g vial")
       - `pnfListed: boolean` (Philippine National Formulary essential list)
       - `konsultaCovered: boolean` (part of PhilHealth Konsulta 21 essential drugs)
     * `PrescriptionRequirement`:
       - `id: string`
       - `title: Record<Language, string>`
       - `description: Record<Language, string>`
       - `mandatory: boolean`
       - `ra6675Citation: string`
   - Pure functions:
     * `filterGenericMedicines(query: string, category?: string): GenericMedicineItem[]`
     * `getPrescriptionChecklist(language: Language): Array<{ id: string; title: string; description: string; mandatory: boolean }>`
     * `getDoctorRequestScript(language: Language, medicine?: GenericMedicineItem): string`
       - Generates polite, respectful script in 4 languages (`taglish`, `cebuano`, `ilocano`, `en`) asking the attending physician for generic name, dosage, and duration.
       - Zero em dashes (`\u2014`).

2. **Interactive UI (`src/components/triage/GenericPrescriptionGuideCard.tsx`)**:
   - Render in `src/components/TriageWizard.tsx` (Step 3: Rekomendasyon at Gabay) and linkable/accessible from Step 2 / `PrintFormsView.tsx`.
   - Fast search / filter by medicine name (brand or generic) and therapeutic category.
   - Displays matched medicines with brand equivalents, PNF/Konsulta badges, and dosage formats.
   - Interactive 6-point Prescription Completeness Checklist:
     1. Complete Doctor Details (Name, PRC License, PTR, Signature)
     2. Generic Name clearly stated (RA 6675 Generics Act requirement)
     3. Exact Dosage Strength and Form
     4. Total Quantity in figures and words
     5. Clear Direction, Frequency, and Duration
     6. Valid Date of Prescription (within validity window)
   - 1-click copy for the polite request script for attending doctors.
   - Full 4-language support (`taglish`, `cebuano`, `ilocano`, `en`).
   - Strict accessibility: touch targets $\ge 44$px, `autoComplete="off"` on inputs, zero em dashes (`\u2014`), zero horizontal overflow on 320px viewport.
